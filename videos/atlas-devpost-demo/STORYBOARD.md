## Video direction

- Palette system: near-black `ink-black` is the primary field, `cream` carries display and body copy, `fire-orange` is the inherited token name for ATLAS mint and remains the only accent. Use the mint register sparingly for declarations, verified states and the final lockup.
- Type system: Avenir Next by display and body roles, SFMono-Regular for data, identities, hashes and cloud labels. Display stays lowercase and dominant, data stays compact and tracked.
- Motion grammar: one paused seek-safe timeline per frame. Use smooth long-tail settles with `power3` as the default, then reveal each item on its spoken cue across the back half. Entrances use explicit `fromTo` states. No infinite motion, randomness, CSS transitions or CSS keyframes.
- Rhythm: Frames 1, 4, 6, 8, 9 and 10 develop across the narration. Frames 3, 5, 7 and 11 finish on deliberate held proof. Frame 12 gives the lockup the longest still read.
- Caption keep-out: all load-bearing content stays inside the top 83% of the frame.
- Negative list: no invented screenshots, browser chrome, real cursors, stock imagery, gradients unrelated to the product, floating AI particles, bouncy entrances, lazy breathing, back-half camera drift, slideshow front-loading or screensaver motion.
